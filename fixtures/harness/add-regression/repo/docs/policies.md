# Customer policies

- Refunds are available within 30 days of purchase.
- Exchanges are available within 14 days of delivery.
- Orders ship within 2 business days and arrive within 5 business days.
- Orders may be canceled before fulfillment begins, but cannot be canceled once fulfillment has
  started or the order has shipped.
- Products include a 1-year limited warranty.
- We do not sell customers' personal data.
